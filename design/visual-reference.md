# Tapaia visual reference: what the book says, and what we chose

*Draft for Phase 1 mockups, Oct 7, 2026.* Sources: the text of *Snowmoon* by Vitalik Buterin (GPL v3), chapter numbers in brackets like [ch6]. Quotes are copied from the novel. Anything the book does not describe is marked **(design choice)** so it never gets mistaken for canon.

Art direction from Angelo: an 8-bit / 16-bit pixel take on a late-90s AOL chat room, cozy and warm (Stardew Valley-*esque* feel: soft saturated colors, wood and stone, foliage, lamp light, chibi sprites, wood/parchment panels). **All art is original**, drawn by `design/mockups/art/build_art.py`; nothing is copied from Stardew Valley or any other game.

---

## 1. Tapaia Square itself

The book's only description of Tapaia Square is one assembly conversation [ch6]:

> "There's a similar square about a kilometer further west, Tapaia Square. It has similar things - you can sit down, drink tea, there are shops nearby. But the playground is much more basic, my son says it's not even comfortable. And I always get the feeling that that one is less lively than Galanar." [ch6]

> "Tapaia's library is also like that. All the books are either some boring business books, or things in foreign languages nobody understands, either Old Belpakian or something from Haragmir." [ch6]

> "There's a rubric that incentivizes ground-floor active use, but a lot of people are gaming the system - putting things in that theoretically qualify but that nobody actually wants to use." [ch6]

The "similar things" it compares against, Galanar Square:

> "I always go there to drink tea and have food and look at the birds and trees. It's such a nice space. Good trees, good tea, good shade, there's always interesting people to talk to coming by." [ch6]
> "There's also a mini library and science museum there!" [ch6]

**What goes in the scene (canon):** seating, tea, shops nearby, a deliberately *basic* playground, a small library (its window shows plain business books and foreign-script spines), trees and shade.
**Our additions (design choice):** a tea cart with a striped awning and outdoor tables, benches, warm lamp posts, flower beds, a red-leaf maple (borrowed from the garden in [ch1]), birds. The "less lively" line is a hook for the project ("we're making Tapaia lively"), so the square is shown fairly quiet with a few citizens rather than crowded.

## 2. Meldan streets, buildings, materials

> "A series of houses made out of large stone bricks lay behind the trees on both sides, medieval in style but without any of the junk, dust on the ground, or other imperfections... A few of the houses featured small signs advertising clothing, flowers or food available for sale. Each house seemed to meld seamlessly into the scattered leaves, plants and trees that surrounded it." [ch1, Kalimar]

> "Most of the houses had one of a few similar default styles, with beige and light blue and grey colors, and appeared to have been constructed within the last twenty years." [ch6] About one in ten has a quirk: a spaceship balcony, refurbished shipping containers [ch6].

> "the blue-grey colored Badra St #1103 ... modern, but tasteful ... footpaths on both sides, and both footpaths were covered by trees" [ch1]
> "one building made with an architectural style mixing wood and stone, another with a garden on the roof, and a third ... shaped like an octahedron ... with a maple tree brimming with red leaves at each corner." [ch1]

> "a mountain covered with trees. A few apartment buildings about fifteen storeys tall, decorated and shaped like over-sized medieval castle towers though with far more windows on the sides, poked up high above the trees" [ch1; also ch32]

> "There were buildings of various styles, some that were aesthetic imitations of something older and almost medieval, and others that were openly modern and futuristic." [ch32, commercial district]
> "a tall soundproof barrier, painted with green and blue colors, barely visible behind another even thicker row of tall trees." [ch1]
> Kalimar: "covered with trees and surrounded by stone houses on both sides" [ch27]; buildings dug into man-made hills that "look like nature" [ch27].

**Applied:** stone-brick shopfronts with small hanging signs (clothes, flowers, food); beige, light-blue and grey house walls; one wood-and-stone building (the tea house); one rooftop garden; the tree-covered mountain on the skyline with castle-tower apartments that have lots of windows; trees in front of everything. **Design choice:** roof colors (slate blue-grey and warm terracotta), exact window shapes, cobblestone paving pattern for the plaza.

## 3. Plants, trees, nature, weather

> "The nature in Veridia is beautiful" [ch1]; "tall green trees" shade every sidewalk [ch1]; "thick green shrubbery" [ch1].
> "Veridia's beautiful ... landscape of green plants" [ch26]. "I like the green here." [ch32]
> Weather in the book's winter months (Snowmoon, Rainmoon) is mild in Meldan: "A chilly breeze" [ch1], "A cold breeze" [ch27], "A gentle cool breeze" [ch20]. Snow is a northern thing: "several months of no green and only snow" [ch32].

**Applied:** green dominates the scene; several tree tones; shrubs and vines. **Design choice:** a clear, soft day with warm late-afternoon light (no season shown, so the art fits any month), puffy clouds, birds.

## 4. Clothing: privacy robes, shoes, neck bands, everyday clothes

> "the same signature Veridian Privacy Robe ... This was a loose-fitting dark purple dress that covered the full body from the hood covering the wearer's head down to the ankles. Below the ankles, privacy robe wearers all wore shoes of the same dark purple color." [ch1]
> "his shoes, which had high heels to raise his height to the same level as other robe wearers and deliberately uneven soles to randomize his gait" [ch1]
> "He pulled on a strap on his privacy robe's front face cover, tightening a belt that separated the area below his nose from the area above his nose." [ch1; also ch11]
> Worn casually too: "wearing a privacy robe without putting on the face cover or hood" [ch6, ch17].
> "The silken cloth band tightly covering Gladias's neck" (the neck band) [ch1]; people whisper through them, others hear via earpieces [ch6].
> Non-robe clothing: "simple shirts, either plain colored or featuring slogans of their favorite bands" [ch1]. Helisport kids in "yellow shirts" and "purple shirts" [ch11].

**Applied to avatars:** three outfits: robe with hood down (face visible), robe with hood up and face cover (with the nose strap drawn as a lighter band), and plain or band shirt. Robes and shoes are dark purple. Robe shoes have a visible high heel and one uneven sole. Optional neck band is a pale silky band at the throat. **Design choice:** chibi proportions; the face cover shows two small eye glints so hooded sprites still read as friendly characters (the book doesn't say whether eyes show); hair and skin options; band-shirt slogans are invented.

## 5. Devices, payments, lights, interiors

> Green circles everywhere: "a circle with a glowing green outline on the entrance gate" [ch1]; "a green circle on top of a one meter tall pole, which was labeled 'Check in'" [ch5]; on tables [ch3, ch6, ch23], beside doors [ch20, ch27], taxi doors [ch20]. A failed payment: "The outline turned red." [ch6]. Approval: "a green checkmark lit up" [ch1]; "lighting around the edge of his seat turned green" [ch8]; Order lights "in a unique green shade" [ch6].
> Indoor air: "faint violet lights shining down from the ceiling ... far-UVC disinfection lights" [ch6]; "some bright yellow lights to light up the room, and a few faint violet-looking lights to keep the air clean" [ch8]; air filters are white boxes [ch3]; "ceiling-mounted fans" [ch6].
> A tea booth: "a cloth curtain protecting it ... a decorated lamp in the middle of the far end of the table, with one large flower on each side" [ch3]. An underground cafeteria with "green trees on all sides. Two of the walls were covered with vines" [ch23].
> Emerald shows urgency by color: "He had only seen messages in this shade of red twice before" [ch23]; "Her watch was flashing bright red" [ch20].

**Applied:** the green-circle glow is our "verify / sign / pay" color (wallet sign-in, arrival burn confirm, verified badges); red outline is the error color; a faint violet is used for "quiet" tints. A green circle on a pole stands in the square by the tea cart. **Design choice:** warm lamp light on posts outside (the book only describes lamps indoors).

## 6. On-screen messages and time (how the book draws chat)

The novel prints device messages as a small table: **From → Message, then a decimal timestamp** [ch1, ch20, ch23]:

> Anonymous / Rep score ≥ 200 Verified ✓ / → I saw you at the concert, I know that you are in the Order. / 60259 [ch1]
> Hreda → Northglade was invaded this morning. 20045 [ch20]

Decimal time: 100 ticks per minute, 100 minutes per longhour, 10 longhours per day [briefing; ch-wide]. The book's own web edition draws "device" screens as a dark navy panel (`#0a0e27`) with light blue monospace text (`#7fbcff`) (from the site's CSS in `/workspace/snowmoon/html/`).

**Applied:** in-character rooms show messages as `Name → text` with a right-aligned 5-digit tick timestamp, like the book. The decimal clock and Emerald tips use the book's navy "device" panel colors. OOC rooms use normal clock times.

## 7. Brands and color cues seen in the city

> Hydrafill: "the classic yellow and green colored Hydrafill bottle" [ch1, ch3, ch11], with ads on outer walls.
> Greater Plum Harbor: "a high grey and purple colored wall" [ch5]; "a mixture of white, grey and purple colors" [ch3].

**Applied:** a small yellow-and-green Hydrafill poster on one wall of the square. GPH is not shown (it's a separate enclave).

## Decor list (for art and UI)

Canon: stone-brick buildings, small shop signs (clothes, flowers, food), beige / light-blue / grey houses, wood-and-stone building, rooftop garden, red maple, trees and shrubs everywhere, the tree-covered mountain with castle-tower apartments, benches, tea, a basic playground, a small library of dull business books and foreign-language books, green circles (on a pole, on tables, by doors), Hydrafill yellow-and-green ads, dark purple robes with hoods, face covers with nose straps, high-heeled uneven-soled purple shoes, silky neck bands, plain and band shirts, violet ceiling lights, white air-filter boxes, the From → Message / tick-timestamp chat format.

Design choices: wood-and-parchment window frames with beveled AOL-style edges, purple title bars, the tea cart's striped awning, lamp posts with warm light, cobblestones, flower beds, birds, puffy clouds, zipcoin as a gold coin with a "Z", the Founding Citizen medal (gold with a purple ribbon and a leaf), chibi sprite proportions, door-creak and chime sounds.

## Sound notes (design choice, for the spec)

AOL's door open / door close sounds become a wooden door creak when someone enters a room and a softer latch click when they leave; a tea-cup "clink" for mentions; a low crackle for a real burn. All sounds are off by default on mobile and can be muted per room.
